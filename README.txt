Ugreen NAS connects your UGREEN NAS to Homey over your local network, without any cloud connection. Follow CPU and memory usage, CPU and disk temperatures, fan speeds, network and disk activity and how full your storage pools are, in the Homey app or with the NAS Status widget on your dashboard. You can also reboot or shut down the NAS from Homey, and use it in Flows, for example to switch it off at night once your backup has finished.

To add your NAS, enter its local IP address, port and the login of a local user with administrator rights. Accounts with two-factor authentication are supported: you enter a code once and Homey is remembered as a trusted device. If your NAS uses HTTPS or sits behind a reverse proxy, enable "Use HTTPS"; self-signed certificates are accepted. If the connection stops working later, for example after a password change, use Device settings > Repair to sign in again without losing your Flows.
